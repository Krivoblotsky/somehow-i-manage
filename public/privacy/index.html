Privacy policy

Your people, your notes, your data.

Effective 1 October 2026

The short version

  • You sign in with Google or Microsoft. We receive your name, email address and, when the account has one, a profile picture, and use them only to tell your account apart and show who is signed in.
  • What you write about your team is stored on your device and in a private database so it follows you between devices. Only you can read it.
  • No ads, no trackers, no third-party analytics, no selling or sharing of data. Ever.
  • You can export everything or delete everything at any time, from inside the app.

Who we are

Somehow I Manage (“the app”, “we”) is a people-first task manager for managers, made and run by an independent developer. It lives at somehowimanage.app. Questions about this policy or your data: hello@somehowimanage.app.

What we collect

  • Your Google or Microsoft account details. When you sign in we receive your name, email address, a profile picture when the account has one, and an account identifier. The app asks for nothing beyond this basic profile: no access to your mail, calendar, files or contacts.
  • What you create. The people you add (names, roles, contact details and photos you choose to enter), and the tasks, notes and 1:1 notes you write about them, plus small things like where a card sits on your map.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the servers that host the app record requests (IP address, time, browser type) in short-lived logs. We run no third-party analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The only thing kept in your browser’s storage is your sign-in session and your own data.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your name and picture in the app, and store and sync your data so it is the same on every device you use. That is all. We do not sell data, share it for marketing, build profiles, or use it to train anything.

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it lives

  • On your device, in the browser’s local database, so the app works offline.
  • In your private sync database, hosted by Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), which also handles the sign-in. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and database rules limit every row to the account that created it.
  • The website itself is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

One optional lookup: when you add an email address to a person, the app asks Gravatar (Automattic, Inc.) whether a profile picture exists for it, sending a hash of that address rather than the address itself. You can replace or remove the picture at any time.

Dictation. If you press the microphone in a field, your browser turns your voice into text: Safari through Apple’s speech recognition, Chrome through Google’s. The audio goes to them for that purpose only; we never receive or keep it, and the words land in the app like anything you type.

AI assistants you connect. If you connect an assistant through our MCP server, it signs in as you and asks for your approval once; from then on it reads and changes your data as you, under the same rules as the app, until you disconnect it in Account & sync. We do not see what you say to the assistant; the assistant's own provider handles that under its own policy. Nothing is connected unless you connect it.

Finding out what works. The landing page counts its visits by day, page and campaign link (the tag at the end of a link you clicked), without cookies, IP addresses or anything about you. After your first sign-in the app may ask once where you heard about it, and after a week two questions about it; what you answer is stored with your account and read only by us, to decide what to build and where to tell people.

The people you write about

Your team never sees what you record, and neither do we. You decide what to write; please keep it to what you need to be a good manager. Everything you add about others stays in your account and can be edited or deleted by you at any moment.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your data stays for as long as you keep using the app.
  • Export everything as a file at any time from the app’s menu.
  • Delete all data in Account & sync removes every person, task and note from this device and from the sync database.
  • Delete your account, also in Account & sync, removes the account itself and everything in it, on every device, at once. If you would rather we do it, email hello@somehowimanage.app and it is done within 30 days.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you. Most of this you can do yourself inside the app; for anything else, write to us and we will help.

Children

The app is a tool for people at work and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. For anything that matters, we also say so inside the app.
